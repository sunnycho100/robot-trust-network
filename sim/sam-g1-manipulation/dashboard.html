Normal mode: learned balance and flat-floor walking, direct arm joints, hand reaching, grippers and crouching. Rig mode: all 29 body joints, with an explicit pelvis support and balance policy off; this does not demonstrate free-standing movement. Running, jumping and climbing are not implemented. Camera-driven actions use no object-specific pickup scripts or hidden object coordinates. Grasping and placement remain experimental; visual verification can be uncertain. Image-only trials receive one image and your question, without object telemetry or previous chat; they are read-only. Scene/seed/camera changes reset the simulation.
